The Psychology of Money

Author: Morgan Housel

Genre: Finance / Personal Development

Review

The Psychology of Money explains how emotions and habits affect financial decisions. It discusses saving, spending, investing and patience. I found the book useful because it gives simple lessons about managing money wisely.
