Wonder

Author: R. J. Palacio

Genre: Fiction / Young Adult

Review

Wonder tells the story of August, a boy with a facial difference who starts school for the first time. The book explores friendship, kindness and acceptance. I liked its simple story and meaningful message about treating everyone with respect.
